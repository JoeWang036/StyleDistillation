The visual style embraces bold, flat color application, blending vibrant hues of green, blue, orange, red, and yellow. Brush strokes are broad and loose, offering a textured, painterly effect. Lighting is diffuse, and the composition is harmonious, with simplified forms creating rhythmic movement. The palette is bright and lively, conveying dynamic energy and an abstract simplification. The style suggests an expressive tone, emphasizing vivid interplay between colors and shapes. Shadows and forms are soft-edged, contributing to an overall sense of fluidity and abstraction.
