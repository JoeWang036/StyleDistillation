The visual style utilizes a loose, free-flowing watercolor technique characterized by soft, diffused edges and varied saturation. The color palette is vibrant, dominated by warm reds and oranges, juxtaposed with cool blues and purples. Brushstrokes are expressive, blending hues smoothly and creating a dynamic, fluid texture. Lighting is gentle, with no harsh contrasts, lending a dreamy, ethereal quality. The composition is balanced yet asymmetrical, maintaining visual interest with layered pigments that suggest depth and movement without rigid outlines.
