The visual style is characterized by bold, clean lines and a bright, saturated color palette dominated by reds and yellows. Shadows and highlights are emphasized through strong, defined contrasts, contributing to a three-dimensional appearance. The illustration uses a traditional comic book technique with minimal texturing, focusing on clarity and vividness. The composition is dynamic with a central focus and employs a perspective that enhances the sense of movement. Soft gradients and subtle shading add depth, while the background employs a wash effect with lighter hues, maintaining visual clarity and balance.
